# 16 — Minor sweep after Milestones 2d and 2e (Milestone 2f)

## Question

Which of the deferred minors of write-ups 14 and 15 does a person meet
(an administrator on the setup page, a tenant user on the login page),
and what does the browser show once they are fixed: the logo above the
realm name, a weak password refused at the first login, a login that
took longer than the ten-minute login state landing on the console
instead of a bare error.

## Measured on

- Prism Central pc.7.6.0.6, Keycloak 26.3.5 on the lab VM
- Portico `dev-4baffcf` (branch `m2f-minor-sweep`; the unit tests and
  the realm policy ran on `dev-673103e`, the browser checks on the
  binaries that followed the review and the first two checks), Rocky
  Linux 9.8
- Date: 2026-10-18
- By: Claude (Marco's session) for the unit tests, the deploy and the
  realm policy; Marco in Chrome on the Mac for the three visual checks

## Method

1. One commit per finding with a test watched failing first (plan
   `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-18-milestone-2f-minor-sweep.md`).
2. Lab: `PUT /admin/realms/portico-lab` with the password policy (the
   realm predates the bootstrap that now sets it), `deploy.sh --fresh`
   (existing store: binary and unit only), branding re-saved through
   `PUT /api/v1/settings/branding` so the theme CSS is regenerated,
   `lab/tenants/acceptance.sh` for tenant B and carol.
3. Chrome: `https://prism.lab.portico.test/` (login page: logo and
   title); carol's first login with a weak password, then a valid one;
   the login page left open for more than ten minutes before signing in.
4. `lab/tenants/acceptance.sh --delete`.

## Result

- MEASURED, unit tests: 18 new or extended tests across `keycloak`,
  `settings`, `tenant`, `api`, `web`, `branding`, `auth` and
  `cmd/portico`; two of them (`TestServiceGetNamesAPlaintextSecret`, the
  refused-import extension) passed before any change and close coverage
  gaps rather than bugs.
- MEASURED, Keycloak: the realm policy PUT answered 204 and `GET
  /admin/realms/portico-lab` reports `length(12) and notUsername and
  notEmail`.
- MEASURED, deploy: `systemctl show portico -p StartLimitIntervalUSec`
  answers `0` after the deploy; the service was back with `mode=running`.
- MEASURED, theme: after the branding re-save the generated
  `login/resources/css/portico.css` on the VM contains
  `#kc-header-wrapper::before` and no `padding-top`.
- MEASURED, Chrome (Marco), login page: the logo sits above the realm
  name. The first pass drew the name white on white: keycloak.v2 paints
  its header for a dark background the Portico theme does not have.
  Fixed in the same branch (`color: #1f2937`,
  `TestThemeCSSGivesTheHeaderADarkColour`); the name is readable but
  plain, a layout pass is in `docs/backlog.md`.
- MEASURED, Chrome (Marco), first login: a short password at the
  "Update password" step was refused with `Invalid password: minimum
  length 12`; a 12+ character one was accepted.
- MEASURED, Chrome (Marco), stale Prism Central session: the first login
  after the tenant had been deleted and recreated landed on Prism
  Central's own `403 Access Denied` page (`/console/access_denied.html`).
  Portico's log shows no SAML round trip: the browser still held the
  `iam-sessionid` cookie of the previous carol (Prism Central's Sign Out
  had failed with 403 after `acceptance.sh --delete`, write-up 15), `/`
  was proxied and Prism Central answered for a project that no longer
  existed. After `/.portico/logout` (which expires the Prism Central
  cookies) the login went through: `POST /api/iam/authn/callback` 303,
  `/dm/` 200, `/dm/v3/groups` 200. Recorded in `docs/backlog.md`.
- MEASURED, latency during that login (Portico's access log reports the
  upstream time): Prism Central's SAML callback **5.1 s** and
  `/auth_code` **4.0 s** (1.2 s in write-up 13: IAM was still digesting
  the tenant delete and recreate of 22:10); a 3.6 MB console stylesheet
  **0.07 s** direct from the VM, **0.16 s** through the proxy's
  pass-through listener on the VM, **0.32 s** direct and **0.27 s**
  through Portico from the Mac over the VPN. The proxy adds about a
  tenth of a second on the largest asset; the rest is Prism Central and
  the VPN.
- MEASURED, Chrome (Marco), logout: `/.portico/logout` landed on
  Keycloak's "Logging out — Do you want to log out?" page, because the
  end-session request carried no `id_token_hint`. Fixed in the same
  branch: the session keeps the raw ID token (memory only, never logged)
  and the logout sends it (`TestLogoutSendsTheIDTokenHint`).
- MEASURED, Chrome (Marco), expired login state: the login page was left
  open from 22:44 and the credentials typed at 22:58. The journal shows
  `callback: login state missing, restarting the login`, `/.portico/
  callback` 303, `/.portico/login` 302, Keycloak answering at once on
  its SSO session, `login carol@lab.portico.test` and the second
  callback 302: the console, one second later, instead of the bare
  `login state missing or expired` page of Milestone 2e.
- MEASURED: the first `lab/tenants/acceptance.sh --delete` afterwards
  reported one FAIL, `_internal project unchanged -> no`, with every
  other line ok; the script discards its two snapshots, so the differing
  field is unknown. A second create-and-delete cycle (tenant B, carol)
  passed on every line, and two snapshots of `_internal` 20 s apart were
  identical. Backlog: print the diff on that failure.

## Implications for the design

- §4.4 now says the realm Portico creates carries the password policy
  and that MFA is a realm setting the MSP turns on in Keycloak.
- A callback without its login state restarts the login once, with a
  marker in the OIDC state (not a cookie, which a browser blocking
  cookies could not hold), so blocked cookies get the plain error
  instead of a loop. Recorded in §4.3.
- Portico's logout sends `id_token_hint`; the session therefore keeps
  the ID token in memory.
- A Prism Central session that outlives a tenant re-provisioning is a
  known gap (backlog): `/` is proxied when the `iam-sessionid` cookie
  is present, and Prism Central answers 403 for the vanished project.
- `docs/backlog.md` holds the older deferred findings (2a–2c) from now
  on, instead of the final recaps in chat.
