# 30 — The first release: the release job and an upgrade from its assets

## Question

Milestone 6 publishes v0.1.0. Before the real tag, does the release
path work end to end: a tag builds the image, the draft release carries
the notes and every asset with its checksum, and an appliance upgrades
in place from those assets alone, keeping its store, its sites and its
tenants?

## Measured on

- Prism Central: pc.7.6.0.6 on both sites (`AZ-IT-MI1`, NTNX-01
  10.23.210.175; `AZ-US-NYC1`, 10.23.210.185)
- Release: tag `v0.1.0-rc.2` on main `7436de5`, workflow run
  40291846827 of `.github/workflows/image.yml`
- Appliance: `10.23.210.191` (write-up 29), from `dev-f37aafa` to
  `0.1.0-rc.2`
- Date: 2026-10-26
- By: Claude; the tag push is Marco's

## Method

1. Marco pushed the annotated tag `v0.1.0-rc.2`. The workflow built the
   image, the OpenSCAP report and the binary tarball and made a draft
   release.
2. `gh release download v0.1.0-rc.2` into an empty directory on the
   Mac, then `sha256sum -c` on the four `.sha256` files.
3. On the appliance, as `admin`: the two tarballs `install-0.1.0-rc.2.tar.gz`
   and `portico-0.1.0-rc.2-linux-amd64.tar.gz` with their checksums
   copied to a fresh directory, `sha256sum -c`, unpacked, then
   `sudo ./deploy/install.sh --binary ./portico`. This is the documented
   upgrade (`docs/install.md`, Updating) with the binary given by hand
   instead of `--release`, which needs a published release.
4. `lab/sites/tenant-login.sh acme --site az-it-mi1` and
   `--site az-us-nyc1` with `PORTICO_SECRETS=~/portico-secrets-appliance`.

## Result

- MEASURED: the run took 48 minutes (08:22:45Z to 09:10:53Z) and made a
  draft pre-release (`prerelease` from the `-` in the tag) whose body is
  `docs/releases/v0.1.0.md`, with eight assets: the qcow2 (1360265216
  bytes), the OpenSCAP report, the binary tarball and the installer
  tarball, each with its `.sha256`.
- MEASURED: the four checksums verify after download, on the Mac and on
  the appliance.
- MEASURED: the installer printed one backup line,
  `/var/lib/portico/backup-dev-f37aafa-20261026-102934` (root, 0700),
  replaced the binary and restarted the services; `portico version`
  reads `portico 0.1.0-rc.2`; `portico`, `keycloak` and `postgres` are
  active.
- MEASURED: the store survived: both sites and the tenant acme are as
  before, and the acme logins on both sites end on 200 at `/console/`
  after the SAML round trip.
- MEASURED: the Mac had no host key for `10.23.210.191`: the VM of
  write-up 29 is a new one and `lab/appliance/deploy.sh` connects with
  `UserKnownHostsFile=/dev/null`. The old keys (in `known_hosts.old`)
  differ from the new ones, as expected for a new VM; the new keys were
  recorded with `StrictHostKeyChecking=accept-new`.

## Implications for the design

None. The tag form `vX.Y.Z-rc.N` (SemVer pre-release, the dot before
the number) goes into `CONTRIBUTING.md` at the close of the milestone.

## How to re-measure after an upgrade

Push a tag `vX.Y.Z-rc.N`, wait for the run, download and check the
draft's assets as in steps 2 and 3, upgrade the appliance as in step 3
and log in as in step 4. Delete the draft release afterwards; the tag
stays.
